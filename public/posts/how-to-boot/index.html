How to boot an OS

August 2, 2026

After you Flash to a USB, you should boot it onto your computer. But how do you do that?

How do I boot to a USB?#

After you etch to your USB of choice, plug it into your computer. Then you will need to enter UEFI, also known as BIOS. You can do this by restarting your computer and spam clicking a specific key (Tip: Look up [Motherboard Name] how to enter BIOS). Then once you are in, drag your USB to the top of boot priority, and save and apply changes.

What happens after I do that?#

Then what happens is that you will see a screen where you choose your operating system to boot to (Sometimes, especially with Ventoy). Then use your arrow keys and enter to boot that operating system, if you need to. Then you are in!
